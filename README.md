
 🛡️ FRAUDGUARD AI

 Digital Public Safety Intelligence & Fraud Detection System

## Overview

FraudGuard AI is a **deterministic, rule-based cyber intelligence simulation system** designed to detect and analyze digital fraud patterns in real time.

It models how modern cyber fraud networks operate using:

* Keyword-based threat intelligence
* Template-based scam detection
* Region-specific vulnerability mapping
* Geospatial risk propagation (ASCII India heatmap simulation)
* Deterministic risk scoring engine (fully explainable AI logic)

The system is designed as a **prototype for government-level cyber defense dashboards** used in fraud monitoring, digital arrest scam detection, and financial fraud prevention.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement Addressed

**AI for Digital Public Safety: Defeating Counterfeiting, Fraud & Digital Arrest Scams**

India faces rapidly growing cybercrime threats including:

* Digital arrest scams
* Phishing & OTP fraud
* Fake RBI / bank impersonation
* UPI & payment fraud
* Social engineering attacks

FraudGuard AI aims to build a **proactive intelligence layer** instead of reactive complaint systems.
